## Long description
Stick Bastion is a wave-defense game where your stickmen do the shooting and you make every call.

Your base has four gunner posts. Tap a post, pick a gun, and the gunner aims and fires on their own. Each post has its own set of guns: blasters, saws and miniguns up front, flamethrowers and rockets on the upper deck, frost rays and lasers on the roof, and railguns and gravity guns up on the high tower. Spend the coins you earn to level guns up to LV 6, repair gunners who get hit, boost their fire rate, call in airstrikes and patch the walls.

Enemies come in from the right: rifle grunts, blade raiders, shield brutes, bomb runners, hover bikes, stealth assassins, healers, mind controllers, bomber drones, juggernauts and more. Each one needs a different answer. Every fourth level ends with a boss, and the Goliath War Mech will test everything you have built.

Career mode takes you through 16 levels across four fortresses: Desert Outpost, Frozen Foundry, Jungle Citadel and Inferno Core. Earn up to three stars per level by finishing it, keeping the base above half health and keeping every gunner standing. You need 10 stars in a chapter to open the next one.

Between battles, spend credits on permanent upgrades and unlock premium guns in the Armory: the Frostbite Rifle, Void Drake, Gravity Lance and Solar Cannon. Come back each day for streak rewards, daily missions and supply drops.

When the career is done, Endless Survival waits. Waves never stop, a boss shows up every five waves, and your best run is saved.

It is hard. That is the point.
